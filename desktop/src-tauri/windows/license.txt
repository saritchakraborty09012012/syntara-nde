Syntara - Terms of Use and Privacy Policy
==========================================

Syntara is a local-first application. It runs AI models that you download
and store on your own device. Please read the following terms before
installing Syntara.

1. The software
---------------

Syntara is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. You are
responsible for evaluating whether Syntara is suitable for your purposes,
and for backing up any data you store with it.

2. Your models and your prompts stay on your device
----------------------------------------------------

Syntara does not require an account. It does not upload your models, your
prompts, your conversations or your files to any server. Inference runs
against model files that you have downloaded or connected yourself, and
those files remain under your control at all times.

3. When Syntara uses the network
--------------------------------

Syntara uses the internet only for the features you ask it to use:

  - downloading a model or an application update;
  - fetching documentation, version metadata or a model catalogue;
  - optional integrations you enable yourself.

Core operation - loading an already downloaded model, generating text,
serving the local API, reading local history and configuration - works
without an internet connection. If a network feature fails, Syntara
reports the failure instead of blocking local use.

No telemetry, tracking or analytics are collected. Nothing about what you
run, ask or generate is reported anywhere.

4. Where your data is stored
----------------------------

Chat history, settings, logs and model metadata are stored locally, in
folders chosen by you or by the location Syntara was installed to. Model
files you connect to Syntara are never deleted without an explicit action
on your part. Removing Syntara does not delete your model files unless you
explicitly choose to delete them during uninstall.

5. The local API
----------------

Syntara can expose a local HTTP endpoint so that other programs on your
machine can use the same model. This endpoint binds to localhost by
default. If you choose to expose it to your local network, that choice is
yours and you are responsible for who can reach it.

6. Third-party components
-------------------------

Syntara bundles open-source runtimes and libraries. Those components keep
their own licenses; the full list ships with the installation.

7. Privacy summary
------------------

  What leaves your device:  only what you explicitly request (a model
                            download, an update check).
  What never leaves it:     prompts, conversations, model files,
                            files you open, usage statistics.
  Accounts:                 none required, none created.

Installing Syntara means you accept these terms. If you do not accept
them, please cancel the installation.
